# 🌐 UWC Bolivia Official Website

## Project Overview

Front-end development and maintenance of the official website for United World Colleges (UWC) Bolivia, a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting international education and understanding. This project serves as the digital gateway for prospective students, parents, volunteers, and supporters of the UWC movement in Bolivia.

<div class="alert alert-info">
<strong>Role:</strong> Front-End Developer | <strong>Organization:</strong> UWC Bolivia | <strong>Status:</strong> Live & Maintained
</div>

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

1. **Information Hub:** Provide comprehensive information about UWC and the Bolivian National Committee
2. **Student Recruitment:** Facilitate the application process for prospective students
3. **Community Engagement:** Connect alumni, volunteers, and supporters
4. **Bilingual Support:** Serve both Spanish and English-speaking audiences
5. **Mobile Accessibility:** Ensure seamless experience across all devices

---

## 🌍 About UWC Bolivia

United World Colleges is a global educational movement that brings together young people from diverse backgrounds to foster peace and sustainability. The Bolivian National Committee is responsible for:

- Selecting Bolivian students for UWC schools worldwide
- Supporting current students and alumni
- Organizing fundraising and awareness events
- Maintaining relationships with donors and partners

## 💡 Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge 1: Bilingual Content Management
**Problem:** Keeping Spanish and English content synchronized
**Solution:** Implemented structured content management system with clear update procedures

### Challenge 2: Limited Technical Infrastructure
**Problem:** Budget constraints for hosting and tools
**Solution:** Utilized free/low-cost solutions and optimized for performance on basic hosting

### Challenge 3: Non-Technical Stakeholders
**Problem:** Content updates required technical knowledge
**Solution:** Created simple CMS-like interface and documentation for easy updates
